Getting Started

How Music Bingo works, for hosts and players.

The idea

Music Bingo is bingo where the squares are song titles. Every player gets a card with a different mix of songs from the host's playlist. The host plays the songs one at a time, and players mark the songs they hear. The first player with five in a row (across, down or diagonal) calls Bingo, and the host checks it.

You can play with phones, with printed paper cards, or both in the same game.

Before your first game

  1. Connect Spotify. On the Host Dashboard, click Connect Spotify and sign in with the Spotify account that will play the music. Playing music needs Spotify Premium. You only need to do this once, unless Spotify asks you to reconnect.
  2. Pick a playlist. It needs at least 24 different songs; around 75 or fewer works best, since bigger playlists make games run long. Include each song only once, because duplicates can cause issues for players. Playlists made by Spotify itself can't be used; make your own copy of the songs in a playlist you own.
  3. Save the playlist under Options → Manage Playlists (Options is in the menu at the top right) to reuse it easily. You can keep it private, or make it public so other hosts can use it too.

Hosting a game

  1. On the Host Dashboard, choose a playlist and click Create Game. You get a 5-character game code and a join link.
  2. Share the link or code. Players open it on their phones and enter their name. They wait in the lobby until you admit them.
  3. Click Admit All to let everyone in, then Start Game. In the Players list every player also has their own Admit button (while they are waiting) and a Kick button, which removes the player, their card and any bingo claim. The player is sent back to the start page and can join again with a different name (handy for an inappropriate name). Players who joined before you started can still be admitted once the game is running; new people can't join a game that has started.
  4. Use Play, Pause and Next to run the music. Embedded (Bingo Host Player) plays in the browser tab you're using, which is the easiest option and the default. To use another device (a phone, speaker or computer), click Refresh devices in the Spotify box: a picker appears if Spotify finds one, and the message under the button says what it found. Play Alone sessions are never offered as a device for a hosted game.
  5. The Played Tracks list shows what has been played, most recent first.
  6. When the game is over, click End Game (the Start Game button turns into End Game once the game has started). Every player's phone then shows Game Over; tapping it takes them back to the start page. The button then becomes Play again, which starts a fresh session of the same game.

Under the Admit All and Start Game buttons you can also turn on Bingo hints for the game; see Bingo hints below. The switches Reveal on QR screen and Music on QR screen turn the QR screen into a song-reveal screen for the room, and can play the music through it; see The QR screen.

Bingo claims

When a player taps Bingo, the claim appears in Bingo Claims on your page. The app checks the card against the songs actually played and tells you whether the line is complete. Then you choose:

  • Accept if it's a real bingo. You then get Continue Game (keep going for more winners) or End Game.
  • Reject if it isn't. The player is told, and play carries on.

For a paper card, type its check code (printed on the card, like 12-KXR) into Check card. You'll see the card, which squares have played, and whether it has a winning line, then accept or reject it the same way.

For players

  1. Open the join link (or go to the site and enter the game code), type your name and join.
  2. Wait for the host to admit you and start the game.
  3. You get a card of song titles. The free square in the middle is already marked. If the host chose an event, its logo (or name) is shown in that square. The card wears the host's theme.
  4. As songs play, the song title is shown on your screen a few seconds in. Tap the matching square on your card to mark it.
  5. Got five in a row? Tap Bingo. The host checks it and accepts or rejects it.

If you close the page by accident, open the same link again on the same phone to get back to your card.

Bingo hints

Cards have B I N G O above the five columns. With Bingo hints on, the letter of one column lights up each time a new song starts, to tell the player where to look.

  • If the song is on your card, the letter of the column it is in lights up.
  • If the song is not on your card, a different column lights up anyway. So a lit letter only means "if this song is on your card, it is in this column". It doesn't tell you whether you have it.
  • Different players can see different letters for the same song, because their cards are different.

For hosts: on your game page, under the Admit All and Start Game buttons, switch on Bingo hints to turn them on for the game. They are off by default. Then choose who gets them:

  • Players choose switched on: each player gets a Hints switch next to their name and turns it on or off for themselves.
  • Players choose switched off: hints are on for everyone.

You can change this at any time, even during a game. Paper cards can't light up, so hints only help phone players and solo players. Hints work best with playlists of 40 songs or more.

Playing alone: use the Bingo hints switch next to the status line. It is remembered for that game on that device.

The QR screen: join code, song reveal and room speaker

Click QR code next to the join link on your game page to open a full-screen page with a large QR code and the game address. Cast it to a TV or projector (or open it on a computer connected to one) so people can scan it and join.

Song reveal for the room. Under Admit All and Start Game, switch on Reveal on QR screen. When you click Start Game, the QR screen turns into a big, themed screen: Game starting in 3, 2, 1, then each song with the same 10-second Song revealed in countdown the phones use, followed by the title and artist in large text. The song before it stays at the bottom as Last song. When a bingo is accepted the screen shows BINGO! and the winner's name with fireworks until you click Continue, and Game over when you end the game. This is handy for people playing with paper cards, who have no phone to see the reveal. It is off by default and you can switch it on or off at any time.

Music through the QR screen's speakers. If you want the sound to come out of the cast device or the room's HDMI/speaker system, switch on Music on QR screen (it is available once the reveal is on). A small 🔊 Play the game music on this screen button then appears in the bottom corner of the QR screen:

  1. On the QR screen, click that button once (browsers won't play sound until a page is clicked). This needs the QR screen to be signed in as you and your Spotify Premium; nothing is installed on that device.
  2. On your game page, click Refresh devices and choose Bingo Room Screen. You can do this before Start Game or during a game, and the music moves there.
  3. You still control everything from your game page: Start Game, Play, Pause and Next.

If you cast a browser tab from your own computer with its sound, the normal Embedded player already plays through the cast, and you don't need this option. If "Bingo Room Screen" doesn't show up, wait a few seconds and click Refresh devices again, or click the button on the QR screen once more.

Paper cards

Hosts can print cards for people without phones, or for a whole crowd.

  • When creating a game, tick Paper cards and choose how many (up to 100 per game).
  • Any game, with or without paper cards, can have an event: choose it in the Event box when you create the game. Its logo (or its name, if it has no logo) goes in the center square of the paper cards and of the phone cards. You set events up under Options → Events: an event needs a name; a logo (PNG or JPG) is optional and, if there is one, it is shown instead of the name.
  • Tick Use my theme's colors on the cards to print them in your theme: the paper stays white (to save ink), and only the lines, the game name and the B I N G O letters are colored; the letters sit in boxes. The choice is saved with the game, so changing your theme later doesn't change cards you already made.
  • On the game page, click Paper cards to open the print page: one large card per page. Click Print.
  • Each card has a number and a check code. Phone cards and paper cards never match each other.
  • Paper players listen to the same music as everyone else and mark their own cards. They call bingo out loud, and you check their card code.
  • Make more adds extra cards to the game.
  • The Card colors switch on the print page turns your theme colors on or off for a game that already has cards. Only how the cards look changes: numbers, squares and check codes stay the same, so cards you've already printed still work.
  • If songs are later removed from the playlist, a notice appears and Update cards swaps only the removed songs on only the affected cards. Reprint those cards. Regenerate all rebuilds every card from the current playlist (the numbers and check codes stay the same, but all printed cards must be replaced). Neither works while a game is being played.
  • If you leave the join footer on, each card shows a QR code and the web address so people can also join on their phone.

Saved games, Game History and Play again

If you run the same game again and again (a weekly family game night, for example), click Save game on its page. Saved games appear under Options → Saved Games, with their game code, playlist and paper cards.

Play again starts a fresh session: the played list, claims and phone players are cleared, but the game code, playlist, event and paper cards stay the same, so cards you've already printed keep working.

Game History (under Options) lists your last 50 games, saved or not, newest first. Each row shows the game code, the playlist, how many players and paper cards it had, the event (with its logo), the card colors if a theme was used, and when it was created. For each game you can Open (or Resume or View) it, Play again, open its Cards, Save it (or Remove from saved), and Delete it. A saved game can't be deleted from here: remove it from saved first, or delete it on the Saved Games page.

Playlists

  • Manage Playlists (under Options) lists the playlists you've saved and public ones from other hosts.
  • Only the host who added a playlist can delete it or change it between public and private.
  • Paste a Spotify playlist link when creating a game or saving a playlist. The page warns you straight away if it's a Spotify-made playlist.

Your account and themes

Open the menu at the top right and choose Options. A menu down the left side has Account, Themes, Saved Games, Game History, Manage Playlists and Events (add, rename and delete events, and add, change or remove their logos). On Themes you can:

  • Pick a theme. Choose from eight ready-made color themes: Midnight Purple (the original), Ocean Blue, Emerald, Sunset, Rose, Graphite, Maize & Blue and Light (dark text on white panels, for bright rooms or if you prefer it). Or pick Custom to choose your own: a color wheel for three colors (background, buttons, highlight; click one, then click or drag on the wheel, and use the Brightness slider or type a color code), and the app works out the panels, text and borders. A preview of a phone card and a paper card updates as you pick. Players see your colors, and paper cards can use them too ("Use my theme's colors"). Cards that were already made keep the colors they were made with. Click a swatch to preview it right away, then click Save theme to keep it. If you leave without saving, nothing changes.

On Account you can change your password (you'll need your current password).

Your theme is used on all your pages and on the games you host: players see it on their phones (the join page, their card and the winner screen), so a game looks the same on every device. Paper cards use it only if you ask (see Paper cards). Pages with no host behind them, like the page where you enter a game code, always use the original look. Each host picks their own theme.

Playing alone

On the home page, choose Play Alone to play by yourself. Songs play through your Spotify and you mark your own card. If you call a bingo by mistake, the app tells you which squares you marked that haven't played.

Tips and troubleshooting

  • No sound: open Refresh devices in the Spotify box and pick your device, or use the embedded player. Make sure Spotify is Connected and that you have Premium.
  • The QR screen is stuck on "Get ready…": the first song hasn't started yet. Click Play on your game page, and check the music is playing on the device you picked.
  • Spotify says it needs reconnecting: click Connect Spotify again on the dashboard.
  • Players seeing an old screen: ask them to refresh the page.
  • A page looks out of date after an update: do a hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R, or Cmd+Shift+R on a Mac) so the browser fetches the new styles.
  • A player joined by mistake or under the wrong name: use Kick next to their name in the Players list. If it is their own phone, they can open the join link again (while the game hasn't started) and tap Not you? Join as someone else to leave and join under a new name.
  • Game code not found: check the code. Codes are 5 letters and numbers, and capitals don't matter.
  • Locked out of your account: ask the administrator to reset your password.
